01. FLUID MASS CONSERVATION & MENISCUS DRAW

Substrate Hydraulic Mass Balance in Fractional Gravity

In low-gravity habitats, buoyancy-driven natural convection ceases, making fluid transport entirely dependent on capillary matric potential $\psi_m$ and active suction from root transpiration $T_c(t)$:

$$\frac{dm(t)}{dt} = \dot{m}_{\text{inj, H}_2\text{O}} + \dot{m}_{\text{inj, nutrient}} - \int_{\mathcal{A}_{\text{leaf}}} T_c(T_{\text{air}}, \text{VPD}, g)\, dA - \dot{m}_{\text{evap}}$$

$$\Delta P_{\text{capillary}} = \frac{2\gamma \cos \theta}{r_p} - \rho g_{\text{eff}} h_{\text{sponge}}$$

02. ELECTROLYTIC CONCENTRATION & RESISTANCE

Bulk Sintered Ceramic Resistance vs. Ionic Solute Molarity

Electrical resistance $R$ across interdigital porous sensors depends directly on the effective conductivity $\sigma_{\text{eff}}$ of the interstitial fluid and volumetric water content $\theta_v$:

$$R(t) = \frac{K_{\text{cell}}}{\sigma_{\text{eff}}(t) \cdot \theta_v^\mu} = \frac{K_{\text{cell}}}{\left( \sum_i z_i F u_i c_i(t) \right) \theta_v^\mu}$$

At dilute concentrations, molar ionic conductivity follows the Kohlrausch limiting law:

$$\Lambda_m = \Lambda_m^0 - \mathcal{K} \sqrt{c_{\text{total}}}, \quad \text{where } c_{\text{total}} = \frac{n_{\text{ions}}(t)}{V_{\text{fluid}}(t)}$$

Closed-Form Jacobian Inversion of $(m, R)$ State

$$\begin{bmatrix} \dot{m} \\ \dot{R} \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \frac{\partial m}{\partial V_{\text{H}_2\text{O}}} & 0 \\ \frac{\partial R}{\partial V_{\text{H}_2\text{O}}} & \frac{\partial R}{\partial n_{\text{ions}}} \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} \dot{V}_{\text{H}_2\text{O}} \\ \dot{n}_{\text{ions}} \end{bmatrix}$$
